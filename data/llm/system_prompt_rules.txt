[all/FGI] FGI-IC-001 | Critical | Infection Control
  DESC: Airborne Infection Isolation (AII) room negative pressure, ventilation, and anteroom requirements
  TRIGGER OCC: Occupied Hospital, Acute Care Hospital
  TRIGGER ROOMS: isolation room, airborne infection isolation, AII, negative pressure room
  TRIGGER SYS: HVAC, exhaust, ventilation
  VIOLATION: Plan review comment — Infection Control: The {occupancy} AII room(s) shown on the submitted drawings do not demonstrate compliance with FGI 2022 §2.1-3.2.6.2 and ASHRAE 170-2021 Table 7.1. Each AII room must maintain a continuous negative pressure differential of not less than 0.01 inches water gauge (2.5 Pa) relative to the adjacent corridor and all contiguous spaces, with a minimum of 12 total air changes per hour (ACH), of which at least 2 ACH must be outdoor air. The mechanical schedules do not clearly indicate exhaust discharge directly to the exterior (minimum 25 feet from air intakes and operable openings), and self-closing, gasketed, or automatic-actuating doors are not specified at all anteroom openings. Anteroom provisions per §2.1-3.2.6.2(d) must be clearly detailed on the architectural plans with positive-latching or automatic-closing hardware to prevent simultaneous opening of both doors.
  FIX: 1. Revise the mechanical schedules and room data sheets to confirm each AII room achieves a minimum of 12 total ACH with not less than 2 ACH outdoor air, and demonstrate continuous negative pressure of -0.01" w.g. relative to all adjoining spaces. | 2. Provide a pressure relationship diagram (air pressure map) for the isolation room cluster showing directional airflow arrows from clean to less-clean spaces, signed and sealed by the mechanical engineer of record. | 3. Detail exhaust duct routing on the mechanical drawings from each AII room exhaust grille to an exterior discharge location at least 25 feet from any air intake, operable opening, or occupied area per ASHRAE 170-2021 §6.2. | 4. Add a dedicated anteroom between each AII room and the corridor. Confirm the anteroom is equipped with self-closing hardware or automatic door operators on both doors and that simultaneous opening of both doors is prevented via an interlock or occupancy sensor per FGI 2022 §2.1-3.2.6.2(d). | 5. Specify a continuous monitoring system (audible/visual alarm) at the AII room entry to alert staff when the required negative pressure differential is not maintained, per ASHRAE 170-2021 §7.1. | 6. Coordinate with the architectural and mechanical drawings to ensure no supply air diffusers are located in a position that would short-circuit room air before it reaches the exhaust grilles.
  REFS: FGI 2022 §2.1-3.2.6.2 | ASHRAE 170-2021 Table 7.1 | ASHRAE 170-2021 §6.2 | ASHRAE 170-2021 §7.1

[all/FGI] FGI-IC-002 | Critical | Infection Control
  DESC: Protective Environment (PE) room positive pressure, HEPA filtration, and separation from AII rooms
  TRIGGER OCC: Occupied Hospital, Acute Care Hospital
  TRIGGER ROOMS: protective environment, bone marrow, transplant, oncology, immunocompromised
  TRIGGER SYS: HVAC, filtration, ventilation
  VIOLATION: Plan review comment — Infection Control: The Protective Environment (PE) room(s) for immunocompromised patients shown in the {occupancy} do not demonstrate compliance with FGI 2022 §2.1-3.2.6.3 and ASHRAE 170-2021 Table 7.1. PE rooms must maintain continuous positive pressure of not less than +0.01 inches water gauge relative to the adjacent corridor and all contiguous spaces to prevent infiltration of airborne contaminants. The submitted mechanical design does not confirm HEPA filtration rated at ≥99.97% efficiency at 0.3-micron particulate upstream of all supply air serving PE rooms, a minimum of 12 total ACH, or that the PE anteroom is not shared with an Airborne Infection Isolation (AII) room anteroom. Sharing an anteroom between a PE room and an AII room is specifically prohibited because the opposing pressure relationships cannot be simultaneously maintained.
  FIX: 1. Revise mechanical schedules to confirm PE room supply air passes through HEPA filters rated at ≥99.97% efficiency at 0.3 micron, installed in a location accessible for filter replacement without contaminating the clean side of the air stream per ASHRAE 170-2021 §6.4. | 2. Confirm and document on the air-handling unit schedule that each PE room receives a minimum of 12 total ACH with continuous positive pressure of +0.01" w.g. or greater relative to all adjoining spaces. | 3. Provide a pressure relationship diagram (air pressure map) signed by the mechanical engineer of record, confirming the PE room is positive relative to its anteroom and the anteroom is positive relative to the corridor. | 4. Verify that no PE room anteroom shares its enclosure or air system with an AII room anteroom; revise the floor plan if a shared anteroom condition currently exists per FGI 2022 §2.1-3.2.6.3(e). | 5. Specify a continuous differential pressure monitoring device with audible/visual alarm at the PE room entry to alert staff when positive pressure is not maintained, per ASHRAE 170-2021 §7.1. | 6. Detail door hardware to confirm self-closing, gasketed doors at both anteroom entries to preserve pressure relationships at all times.
  REFS: FGI 2022 §2.1-3.2.6.3 | ASHRAE 170-2021 Table 7.1 | ASHRAE 170-2021 §6.4 | ASHRAE 170-2021 §7.1

  TRIGGER ROOMS: operating room, OR
  TRIGGER SYS: HVAC, ventilation, filtration

Provide a reflected ceiling plan for each OR showing the low-velocity laminar-flow HEPA diffuser array dimensions, coverage area, and centerline alignment with the surgical table per FGI 2022 §2.1-3.6.3.1. Diffuser discharge velocity shall not exceed 25-35 FPM at 6 inches below diffuser face. | 4. Confirm on the mechanical drawings that OR supply air is not recirculated through the general system; exhaust/return air must be taken from low sidewall registers away from the operative field per ASHRAE 170-2021 §7.1. | 5. Coordinate the structural and mechanical drawings to confirm no obstructions (light fixtures, booms, or structure) interrupt the unidirectional flow pattern over the operative field.
  REFS: FGI 2022 §2.1-3.6.3 | FGI 2022 §2.1-3.6.3.1 | ASHRAE 170-2021 Table 7.1 | ASHRAE 170-2021 §7.1

If the existing footprint cannot achieve minimum area, revise the floor plan to increase room dimensions or relocate fixed equipment to code-compliant alcoves outside the OR clear area. | 4. Confirm 3-foot minimum clearance at all sides of the surgical table within the clear area calculation per FGI 2022 guidance. | 5. Coordinate with medical equipment planner to confirm that boom, pendant, and overhead light positions are accounted for in the room layout without encroaching on minimum clear area dimensions.
