  VIOLATION: Plan review comment — Electrical / Life Safety: The Essential Electrical System (EES) design shown in the {occupancy} one-line diagram does not demonstrate required branch separation per NFPA 99-2021 §6.4.2. The EES must be divided into three (3) separate branches — Life Safety Branch, Critical Branch, and Equipment Branch — each served by a dedicated, separate automatic transfer switch (ATS) and distribution panel. No loads from one branch may be served by the ATS or panel of another branch. The submitted one-line diagram indicates that life safety and critical branch loads share a single ATS or that equipment branch loads are commingled with life safety branch circuits, which is a direct violation of NFPA 99-2021 §6.4.2.2.   FIX: 1. Revise the one-line electrical diagram to show three separate ATSs — one for the Life Safety Branch, one for the Critical Branch, and one for the Equipment Branch — each individually connected to the generator bus per NFPA 99-2021 §6.4.2. | 2. Prepare a branch load assignment schedule listing every circuit, panel, and load by branch type; confirm no life safety loads (egress lighting, fire alarm, smoke control, elevator recall) are on the critical or equipment branch, and vice versa. | 3. Remove any shared ATS or cross-branch distribution from the one-line diagram; provide separate distribution panels for each branch fed from their respective ATS. | 4. Label all EES panels, ATSs, and branch circuits consistently on the one-line diagram, riser diagram, and panel schedules using the nomenclature 'LS' (Life Safety), 'CR' (Critical), and 'EQ' (Equipment) per NFPA 99-2021. | 5. Confirm the panel schedules show no mixing of branch loads within any single EES panel, and that each panel is clearly labeled by branch type at its installation location. | 6. Coordinate with the mechanical and plumbing drawings to verify that all motor loads (HVAC, medical gas compressors, sterilizers) served by the EES are assigned to the Equipment Branch, unless they serve life safety or critical functions as defined by NFPA 99-2021 §6.4.2.
